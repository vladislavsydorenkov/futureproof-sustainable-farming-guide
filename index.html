01 / THE CHALLENGE

Farming through uncertainty.

Ukrainian agriculture is operating under extraordinary pressure: war, rising input costs, disrupted logistics, climate volatility, moisture shortages and the degradation of productive soils.

Yet innovation does not have to mean taking greater risks. This guide turns research into decisions farmers can test on their own land.

It brings together field trials, farm economics, scientific evidence and the practical experience of Ukrainian farmers to identify technologies that can make farms more resilient, resource-efficient and profitable.
Evidence into action

Don't adopt innovation because someone tells you to. Test it. Measure it. Make it earn its place.

02 / WHAT WE TESTED

Four challenges. Practical responses.

01

Reduced tillage

Reduce machinery passes, fuel consumption and labour requirements while protecting soil structure and conserving moisture.

02

Biological products

Explore how beneficial microorganisms can support crop performance, nutrient availability, soil health and farm economics.

03

Biochar

Test a long-term investment in soil capable of improving moisture retention, nutrient availability and resilience.

04

War-damaged soils

Examine pathways for safely restoring agricultural land affected by physical degradation and contamination resulting from hostilities.

03 / WHY IT MATTERS

Ukraine's farms cannot wait for perfect conditions.

Innovation under wartime conditions is not an abstract exercise. It is about protecting margins, conserving scarce resources, rebuilding damaged soils and ensuring that Ukrainian agriculture remains productive through one of the most difficult periods in its history.
